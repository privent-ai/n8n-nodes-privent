# Negative corpus — business-shaped prose that contains NO personal data.

# PROVENANCE. Every line below was hand-authored for this repository, in this
# session, by the assistant, for the N4-7b measurement. Nothing is copied from a
# customer, a real mailbox, a real ticket, or any external dataset. There is no
# generator behind it, deliberately: a corpus we cannot re-run is worse than one
# we can read, and this one can be diffed line by line.
#
# WHY IT EXISTS. The first negative corpus is this repository's own prose and
# source — technical English about detection. It structurally contains no
# correspondence, no invoices, no meeting notes, no support threads. Zero hits
# there says little about the text customers actually send, which is what a local
# detector meets in production.
#
# CONSTRUCTION RULE, applied to every line: business-shaped, and free of anything
# that identifies a person or an account. No names, no addresses, no mailboxes,
# no phone numbers, no card or bank identifiers, no government IDs, no usernames.
# What IS present on purpose: dates, money amounts, percentages, quantities,
# durations, version strings, product and feature names, generic role words —
# exactly the material that produced false positives before, and exactly the
# material a detector must learn to leave alone.
#
# Any detector that fires on a line below is producing a false positive, by
# construction. Lines starting with '#' are comments and are not measured.
